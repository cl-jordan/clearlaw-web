Why this one is different

Every other tool makes you efficient. Then efficiency eats your revenue.

Firms that bill by the hour face a trap: the more the AI does, the fewer hours there are to bill. So the tools that promise speed quietly threaten the business model. LitigationOS is built around the opposite premise.

The trap

A generic AI tool drafts the motion in seconds — and now there's nothing to bill for those hours. Adopt it firm-wide and you've automated away your own revenue, with no defensible way to charge for the judgment you still apply. That's why careful firms hesitate, and why the ones being pushed hardest — insurance defense, squeezed by carriers on efficiency — feel it most.

The LitigationOS answer

Your bar rules make you responsible for whatever goes out under your signature — AI-assisted or not. That verification is real, billable work. LitigationOS does the work inside a per-case workspace that captures the time you actually spend: reviewing, correcting, deciding strategy. You bill for judgment instead of typing, move the tedium off your plate, and stay whole. Faster and defensible — with an audit trail to prove it.

One workspace for the whole matter — not a drawer of point tools.
