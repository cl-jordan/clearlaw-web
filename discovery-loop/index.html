How it works

Four steps, and the hardest one runs itself.

Discovery Loop starts where other tools stop — not after you've collected the client's answers, but at the job of getting them in the first place.

1

Send us the set

Upload the incoming discovery request and whatever case-file content you have on hand — all of it, some of it, or none of it. No setup, no migration, no IT. Cross-check accuracy scales with whatever you provide.

2

Your client answers from their phone

We translate the formal requests into plain-language questions, consolidate the duplicates, and text your client a link they finish on their own time — in multiple sittings, in their own language, with document upload. Unanswered questions stay visible until they’re answered, so nothing silently stalls.

3

We catch the contradictions

As answers come in, Discovery Loop cross-checks them against your case file and flags contradictions and potentially case-damaging admissions — before they ever reach opposing counsel, not after.

4

You get a service-ready draft

Everything compiles into a rules-compliant response — objections, formatting, and your firm's letterhead included — with special handling for high-risk question types like requests for admission. Ready for your review and signature.

Why Discovery Loop is different

Matter management tracks your deadlines. Drafting tools format your objections. Neither one gets the answers out of your client.

That gap is the whole point. Here's what Discovery Loop does that the tools on your desk today don't.

Pillar 01

The client-answer bottleneck, solved

The slowest part of discovery has never been the drafting — it's getting a confused or reluctant client to answer. Discovery Loop lets clients complete their questions in plain language, consolidated to remove duplicates, in their own language, on their phone, across multiple sessions.

Pillar 02

Contradictions caught before they cost you

Client answers are automatically cross-checked against the case file, surfacing contradictions and damaging admissions before the response is served — not after opposing counsel finds them for you.

Pillar 03

Built for attorneys who aren't AI power users

Discovery Loop is built around the rules of civil procedure, with dedicated logic for high-risk question types — the kind of setup you'd otherwise have to be a serious power user to replicate by hand, teaching the rules and loading case context every time.

Pillar 04 · for hourly firms

Speed without losing the bill

Faster discovery shouldn't mean lost revenue. Discovery Loop captures the judgment-based review time the rules require you to log, and makes it easy to context-switch into other billable work while the model runs — turning idle minutes into productive, billable time.

Common questions

What attorneys ask before they try it.

“We already use Clio / MyCase / Filevine.”
Discovery Loop doesn't replace your matter-management platform, and doesn't ask you to move off it. It solves the one thing those platforms aren't built for: getting usable answers out of the client. Keep what tracks your cases — add the piece that fills them in.
“Is this going to hallucinate and get me sanctioned?”
It's built on the rules of civil procedure with specific logic for high-risk question types like requests for admission — not a general-purpose chatbot you have to teach the rules to. And every output is attorney-reviewed and logged before anything is served. Nothing leaves your desk without your signature.
“I'd have to dig up and upload all my case files.”
You don't have to. The cross-check works with as much or as little of the case file as you have on hand — accuracy scales with what you give it. Dropbox already works — point Discovery Loop at your case folder and it pulls the file from there. That covers Clio firms using Dropbox document storage too, since Clio keeps those matter folders in your Dropbox. Native Clio and Box integrations are on the roadmap; and you can always just upload what’s convenient.
“I don't want to deal with any kind of IT setup.”
There isn't any. Open a case, upload the request and whatever case file you have, and generate the client link. It works with the file storage, email, and calendar you already run on — no migration, no configuration, no IT department required.
“Is the contradiction memo discoverable?”
The memo goes to you and only you — never to your client, never filed, never served. It exists for your case analysis, in the same posture as an internal memo you'd draft yourself, and how you hold it within your privilege and work-product framework stays your call. Our job is making sure it never travels anywhere but your desk.
